# Session 4.6 — Memo settlement ("Details of report"), saved figures, memo print, Truck-wise

## Goal
After the loading clerk ticks the LRs and fills the memo header, a **Details of report** window comes up before anything
is saved. The clerk types the **hamali rate per article** and the **commission %**, sees the net and the round figure,
and saves (and prints). The memo keeps the rate, the % and the figures. Saved memos, the memo print, History and the
Truck-wise report show them. Mockup: https://claude.ai/artifact/TEHZ8T2aW9WdWYVJnA6J4M (board **Option A**; Saved memo;
Memo print). Old paper: `docs/reference/old-memo-last-page.jpg`. Runs **after 4.5** is merged and pushed.

## Decided (owner, 2026-11-12)
- **The settlement**, from the memo's frozen totals:
  - Total articles = Σ quantity · Paid = Σ paid · To pay = Σ to pay · Wasuli = Σ wasuli
  - Hamali less = articles × hamali rate
  - Total 1 = to pay − hamali less (**paid is not added**)
  - Commission less = commission % × (to pay + paid), round half up to the paisa (DM's rule, `firmLedger.cjs`)
  - Total 2 = total 1 − commission less · Net = total 2 + wasuli
  - **Round figure** = net to the rupee, **.50 away from zero, the same as DM's `roundRupee`**
    (10,240.50 → 10,241 · 10,240.49 → 10,240 · −120.50 → −121)
  - Check: 177 articles, paid 220, to pay 6,953, wasuli 4,076, rate 0.40, 10 % → 70.80, 6,882.20, 717.30, 6,164.90,
    10,240.90 → **10,241**
- **Inputs:** both boxes **start empty on every memo (no default)**. **0 is allowed**, but the clerk must type it (empty is
  refused). Rate is rupees with at most 2 decimals. % is 0 to 100 with at most 2 decimals. The keeper parses and checks
  both; the screen only shows what came back.
- **A negative total or net is allowed** and is saved, shown and printed with a minus sign.
- **Truck hire and loading hamali** stay in the memo header and are **not** in the net.
- **Window = Option A** (two panels): totals from the ticked LRs plus the two boxes on the left, the settlement steps
  and a big round figure on the right. Figures update as the clerk types.
- **Buttons:** **Save & print (F4)** (main, orange) and **Save only**. **Esc** goes back to the ticked LRs with nothing saved.
- **Changing the rate or % of a saved memo:** **System PIN** (like Edit header), the same window with the two values filled
  in, `version` checked (stale refused), one History line old → new.
- **Print:** the memo print gets the old software's table (Consignor, Consignee, LR No, Particulars, Pak, Paid, To pay,
  Wasuli, then a totals row) and the **Details of report** block after the last row, with "(177 × 0.40)" and "(10 %)"
  printed next to the two amounts.

## Rules for this slice
- **Migration v5** (never edit v1–v4) adds to `memos`:
  - `hamali_rate_paise` (per article) and `commission_bp` (% × 100), both NULL for a memo saved before v5
  - `frozen_wasuli_paise`, backfilled in v5 from each memo's LRs
  - `frozen_net_paise` and `round_rupees`, NULL before v5. These two **may be negative**, so they do **not** use the
    `paise()` helper (its CHECK is ≥ 0).
- **One function**, `memoSettlement(frozenTotals, ratePaise, commissionBp)` in `shared/`, gives every step and the round
  figure in integer paise. The keeper stores `frozen_net_paise` and `round_rupees` from it (frozen memo totals, rule 8's
  exception), so SQL reads them and there is **no SQL twin**. The screen and the print show the steps from the same
  function. The money functions that refuse negative paise stay as they are; only this function and its formatter allow a minus.
- **`memos.create`** takes `hamaliRate` and `commission` (typed text) with the header and the ticked LRs, in the same one
  transaction that issues the challan number. If the header is refused, the window closes and the marked boxes show, as today.
- **New route `memos.editSettlement(id, version, rate, commission, systemPin)`**: it recomputes from the memo's
  **frozen** totals (what was printed), not the LRs now. A memo saved before v5 can get its first settlement this way.
- **Recalculate (Admin PIN)** refreshes the frozen totals, including wasuli, and recomputes net and round figure with the
  **same** rate and %. **Differs** also compares wasuli.
- **Saved memo view:** a Details of report card (rate and % chips, the steps, the round figure) and an **Edit rate /
  commission** button behind the System PIN. A pre-v5 memo shows "No details of report yet" with the same button.
- **Truck-wise report:** columns Date, Challan, Truck, To, LRs, To pay, Paid, Wasuli, **Round figure**, Truck hire, Loading
  hamali. Totals row; frozen figures as today; it must still agree with the other reports on To pay and Paid.
- **History:** `memos.create` says the rate, the % and the round figure. `memos.settlement` says "hamali rate 0.40 → 0.50,
  commission 10 % → 8 %, round figure 10,241 → 10,180".
- Window at 1024 × 768 with the taskbar; Enter goes rate → % → Save & print; Shift+Tab goes back; F4 = Save & print,
  set in Settings like the other keys.
- All text in `src/strings.ts`. `KEEPER_VERSION` bump. Version **0.5.0**.

## Tests
- `memoSettlement`: the check example gives exactly 10,241. .50 cases both signs (10,240.50 → 10,241, −120.50 → −121,
  10,240.49 → 10,240). Rate 0 and % 0. % 100. A negative total 1 and a negative net. Commission rounding half up at the paisa.
- Input parsing: empty refused for both; "0" accepted; 3 decimals refused; % over 100 refused; letters refused.
- dbtest: create stores the rate, %, frozen wasuli, net and round figure equal to `memoSettlement` over odd amounts; a
  retried create with a changed rate is refused (O3); editSettlement with a stale version is refused; Recalculate after
  an LR edit keeps the rate and % and changes net; Differs turns on when only wasuli changed; migration v5 on a v4 book
  backfills `frozen_wasuli_paise` and leaves the others NULL.
- Reports: Truck-wise round-figure total = Σ `round_rupees`; cross-report agreement still holds.
- Print snapshot: memo with the new table and the Details block; a negative net prints with a minus.
- Real-Electron: F4 opens the window, Esc leaves nothing saved, Save & print saves once and prints.
