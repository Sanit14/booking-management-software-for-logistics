# Session 3.5 — Firm Accounts (like DM: list, KPIs, growth chart, top lists, statement)

## Goal
Firm Accounts for every firm in the masters (regular parties), **like DeliveryManager's Firms pages**: a firm
list, and per firm its account for a chosen period — KPI cards with growth against the previous period, a
month-by-month growth chart, top lists, its LRs and memos, and a printable statement. Read-only: built from LRs,
memos and wasuli that already exist. Settlements / balances carried forward are a later, owner-defined step.

Look first (behaviour and layout only, do not copy code): DM `src/pages/Firms.tsx`, `src/pages/FirmDetail.tsx`
(`Kpi` with "vs before", `GrowthChart` — percentages only, `TopList`, period picker),
`electron/keeper/firms.cjs` (`previousPeriod`, one read model per page), `src/components/FirmSummaryPrint.tsx`.

## Decided rules for this slice
**Keeper (reads only; `KEEPER_VERSION` bump; no migration)**
- `firms.accounts(filters, cursor)` — every firm (active and inactive), name / station / phone, LRs as
  consignor, LRs as consignee, last LR date; filter by text (`nameKey` contains), station, "has LRs in period";
  ordered by name; keyset 100 a page. Counts from one SQL with `GROUP BY` over indexed columns.
- `firms.statement(firmId, from, to, role, cursor)` — role = consignor | consignee | both. Rows: the firm's
  non-cancelled LRs in the period (LR no., date, role, other party, From → To, particular, qty, to pay, paid,
  charges, service tax, total, status Booked / Loaded with challan no., wasuli amount and its state:
  unassigned / assigned / collected amount). Ordered by (date, LR no.); keyset 100 a page.
  **Totals for the whole period from SQL** (LRs, qty, to pay, paid, charges, service tax, total, wasuli owed,
  wasuli collected) — per role and combined. Same amount definitions as `lrAmounts` (one SQL twin, tested
  against it as in 3.1).
- `firms.statementTop(firmId, from, to)` — top 5 other parties, top 5 To stations, top 5 trucks (via memos), each
  with LR count. Small `GROUP BY`s.
- `firms.kpis(firmId, from, to, role)` — LRs, memos (trips) its LRs rode on, quantity, freight (to pay + paid),
  total, wasuli collected — for the period **and** the previous period of the same length (DM's
  `previousPeriod`), with growth % (null when the previous value is 0).
- `firms.months(firmId, year, role)` — 12 months: LR count and freight per month and growth % against the month
  before; the chart shows **percentages only** (as DM).
- Walk-in parties have no firm row and do not appear here (they are found by LR search).
- `print.firmStatement(...)` for the print engine of 3.3.

**Screen — Firm Accounts page (Admin group)**
- Left / top: firm list with search, station filter, "Show inactive"; columns Firm, Station, As consignor,
  As consignee, Last LR. Enter / click opens the statement. Ctrl+K firm pick (2.5) can open it ("Account"
  action next to "Open in Masters").
- Statement: period picker (Today, This month, Last month, This year, Custom from–to; default This month), role
  tabs Both · As consignor · As consignee, KPI cards with "▲/▼ n % vs before" (LRs, Trips, Freight, Total,
  Wasuli collected), the 12-month growth chart with a year picker (inline SVG bars, up green / down red, no
  chart library), then the LR table — this page
  may show money (the no-money rule is for the Dashboard only), the LR table with Load more, totals strip, the
  three top lists, **Print** and **Save PDF** (A4 statement: company header, firm, period, rows, totals; pages
  split and repeated header as the memo).
- Clicking an LR opens it in LR Entry view mode (2.3); a challan number opens the memo (3.2).
- Fits 1024×768; the table scrolls in its own box.
- All text in `src/strings.ts`.

## Tests
- Database: list counts per role, filters, inactive, order, paging; statement rows per role, cancelled LRs left
  out, period edges (from / to inclusive, across 1 January), totals = sum of `lrAmounts` of the rows, wasuli
  states and amounts, paging 100 / 101; top lists; EXPLAIN (no scan of `lrs`); speed: 3 000 firms list < 100 ms,
  a statement over 20 000 LRs for one firm < 100 ms.
- KPIs: previous-period dates, growth %, null on a zero base; months: 12 rows, growth vs the month before.
- Print: statement pagination and real PDF (same harness as 3.3).

## Out of scope
Firm ledger, settlements, opening balances, TBB / credit limits (need the owner's rules), reports (4.1).

## Done when
1. All tests pass inside `npm run check`.
2. Real window: Firm Accounts → find a firm → This month → Both / As consignor / As consignee totals match the
   LRs typed for it; KPI growth matches last month by hand; the chart shows 12 months; click an LR → LR Entry
   view; click a challan → the memo.
3. Print / Save PDF of a statement with 60+ rows → 2+ pages, header repeated, totals on the last page.
4. `HANDOFF.md` updated; changed files listed with one suggested commit message and the worktree steps
   (commit and confirm before removing — never `--force`).
